Chicago Winter Running

Where to Run

  • Lakefront Trail — 18 mi paved. Wind off the lake but well-maintained.
  • 606 Trail — elevated, wind-sheltered, 2.7 mi. Good for tempos.
  • Treadmill — no shame. Set 1% incline. Norwegian 4×4 works great on treadmill.

Gear by Temp

50-60°FShorts + long sleeve
40-50°FTights + long sleeve. Light gloves.
25-40°FTights + long sleeve + wind vest. Gloves. Beanie. Buff.
Below 25°FFull tights + base layer + wind jacket. Mittens. OR: treadmill.

Dress like it's 15-20°F warmer. If you're comfortable standing outside, you're overdressed.

Treadmill Guide

  • 1% incline always — compensates for no wind resistance.
  • Strides: Ramp speed to 9.5-10.5 mph, hold 10 sec, step onto rails, rest 30-45 sec. Repeat 4-6×.
  • Norwegian 4×4: Set pace, watch HR. Adjust speed to stay 165-175 bpm. Rails for recovery at 4.5-5 mph.
  • Hill sprints: 8-10% incline, 9-10 mph, 10-12 sec. Grab rails, step off. Rest 60-90 sec.
  • Tempo: Set exact pace and hold. Easier to pace than outdoor.
  • Ice outside = treadmill. A slip = weeks of missed training.

Glossary

Plain-English explanations of every term.

Workout Types

  • Intervals / Repeats (e.g. "5×800m"): Run a distance fast, jog to recover, repeat. "5×800m" = run 800m hard, jog 2 min, do it 5 times total.
  • Tempo run: Sustained "comfortably hard" pace. Can say short phrases but not sentences.
  • Easy run: Relaxed jog, full conversation. Should feel genuinely easy.
  • Strides: 100m smooth accelerations to ~90% effort. Walk back. Not sprints — "fast and relaxed."
  • Hill sprints: 10-12 sec ALL OUT up a steep hill. Walk down. Builds raw power.
  • Norwegian 4×4: 4 × 4 minutes at 90-95% max HR (165-175 bpm) with 3 min jog recovery. HR-based, not pace-based.
  • Fast finish long run: Most of the run easy, last 2-3 miles at a harder pace. Teaches running strong when tired.

Pace & Effort

  • Pace (e.g. "6:07/mi"): Minutes per mile. Lower = faster.
  • WU / CD: Warmup / Cooldown. Easy jogging before/after the hard part.
  • Recovery jog: Very slow jog (or walk) between hard reps. Let HR come down.
  • RPE: 1-10 effort scale. Easy=4-5, Tempo=7, Intervals=8-9, Race=9-10.
  • Negative split: Running the second half faster than the first. The goal.

Recovery

  • Foam rolling: Roll muscles on a foam cylinder. 30-60 sec per area: quads, IT band (outer thigh), calves, glutes.
  • Hip flexor stretch: Kneel on one knee, push hips forward. Hold 90 sec each side.
  • Taper: Reducing volume before a race so you arrive fresh. You'll feel restless — that's it working.
