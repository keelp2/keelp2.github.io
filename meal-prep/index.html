🗓️ Sunday Cook Day

~1 hour hands-on · Oven + Instant Pot + Rice Cooker

0:00 — Start
Preheat oven to 425°F
Season 4 lbs chicken thighs (salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika). Lay on sheet pan lined with foil. Start rice cooker with 4 cups dry rice + water.
0:05
Prep sweet potatoes
Cube 10 sweet potatoes into 1-inch pieces. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil + salt on second sheet pan.
0:10
Everything in the oven
Chicken pan + sweet potato pan in oven. Set timer for 25 min. Start browning ground turkey on stovetop with cumin + chili powder + garlic powder.
0:25
Turkey done
Transfer browned turkey to storage containers. Steam frozen broccoli + peas (microwave bags or stovetop).
0:35
Oven done
Pull chicken + sweet potatoes. Check chicken internal temp (165°F). Let rest 5 min, then slice or shred. Rice should be finishing.
0:45
Pack containers
Portion into 10 meal containers:
• 5 lunch: 6 oz chicken + 1.5 cups rice + broccoli + handful spinach
• 5 dinner: 6 oz turkey + 2 sweet potatoes + ½ cup rice + peas + handful spinach
0:55
Make overnight oats
5 mason jars: 1 cup oats + 1 scoop whey + 1 tbsp PB + 2 tbsp flaxseed + water. Top with ½ cup frozen berries (they thaw overnight). Lid on, fridge.
1:05
Clean up. Done. ✅
~1 hour total. You now have 5–6 breakfasts, 5–6 lunches, 5–6 dinners ready to grab.
